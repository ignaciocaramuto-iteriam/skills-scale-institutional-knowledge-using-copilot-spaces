# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Collaborates With
- Product Managers, Project Managers, QA/Testing, UX Designers, Business Analysts

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Collaborates With
- Project Managers, Developers, UX Designers, Business Analysts, Customer Support Representatives, Stakeholders

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Collaborates With
- Product Managers, Developers, Scrum Masters, Business Analysts, QA/Testing, Stakeholders

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers create intuitive, user-centered interfaces and experiences. They collaborate with Product and Development teams early in the planning phase to ensure solutions meet user needs and usability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria related to user experience
- Validate design solutions with stakeholders and users
- Ensure consistency across user interfaces and journeys

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Incorporate user feedback into iterative design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with product and engineering
- User research findings and usability test reports
- Design specifications and component libraries

### Collaborates With
- Product Managers, Developers, Project Managers, QA/Testing, Customer Support Representatives

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and champion continuous improvement practices. They enable teams to self-organize and deliver efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and reviews
- Identify and help remove impediments to progress
- Coach team members on Agile practices and mindsets
- Track and report on team velocity and delivery metrics
- Support organizational adoption of Agile methodologies

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Reduce process friction and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning agendas and retrospective summaries
- Velocity reports and burndown charts
- One-on-one coaching and team huddles

### Collaborates With
- All team roles (Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, Designers, Analysts)

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, clarify, and document business and user requirements. They bridge business stakeholders and technical teams to ensure solutions align with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business and user requirements
- Create detailed acceptance criteria and use cases
- Map business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Validate that solutions meet stated business goals
- Facilitate communication between business and technical teams

### Goals
- Ensure requirements clarity and completeness
- Reduce rework and scope creep
- Improve alignment between business needs and technical solutions

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and use case specifications
- Process flow diagrams and business context briefings
- Acceptance criteria validation and sign-off

### Collaborates With
- Product Managers, Project Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, Stakeholders, Customer Support Representatives

---

## Customer Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support Representatives provide critical user perspective and feedback. They relay pain points, feature requests, and real-world usage patterns to inform product decisions and validate releases.

### Responsibilities
- Summarize customer issues, trends, and feature requests
- Provide user perspective in planning and retrospectives
- Participate in post-release validation and smoke testing
- Support beta testing and early access programs
- Document gaps between expected and actual user behavior

### Goals
- Reduce support burden and customer churn
- Ensure solutions address real customer pain points
- Improve time-to-value for customers

### Typical Communication
- Customer issue summaries and trend reports
- Product feedback and feature request prioritization
- Post-release support readiness briefings

### Collaborates With
- Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, Project Managers, UX Designers, Stakeholders

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies, acceptance criteria validation, and risk-based quality assurance. They work throughout the project lifecycle to prevent defects and ensure customer confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute test plans and test cases
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Perform functional, regression, integration, and smoke testing
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Recommend quality improvements and risk mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality software that meets user expectations
- Minimize critical defects reaching production
- Maintain customer trust and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics
- Release readiness assessments

### Collaborates With
- Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, UX Designers, Business Analysts, Customer Support Representatives

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups with vested interest in project outcomes. They provide business context, strategic guidance, and approval authority.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and strategic context
- Review and approve major decisions and deliverables
- Escalate risks and issues as needed
- Provide feedback and validate solutions
- Support resource allocation and removal of organizational blockers

### Goals
- Ensure project alignment with business strategy
- Maximize ROI and business value delivery
- Maintain governance and compliance

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Decision logs and escalation communications
- Executive briefings and stakeholder presentations

### Collaborates With
- Project Managers, Product Managers, Business Analysts, and all delivery team members as needed

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Consider the collaboration matrix to identify hand-offs and communication touchpoints in your project planning.
- When assigning work or responsibilities, reference the role descriptions to ensure clarity and reduce ambiguity.
